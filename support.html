Frequently Asked Questions

Is BabyWyze medical advice?

No. BabyWyze provides informational insights only and should not replace professional medical advice. Always consult your pediatrician for health concerns.

How can I delete my data?

You can delete your account and associated data directly inside the app in Settings → Data → Delete Account.

Is my baby’s data secure?

BabyWyze uses secure connections and safeguards to protect your information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
